Assess your Shoulder Health

Hey there! This health assessment tool is designed by our experts. We will guide you through a series of movements and ask you to report your observations. These movements are carefully curated to assess various key aspects of your musculoskeletal health. Some of them will need to be performed lying on the ground or sitting on a chair/stool. For certain movements, you will need to record the time, and the timer on your mobile device can be helpful for this.
Based on your responses, this assessment tool will generate your health score on a scale of 1 to 10. When taken periodically, this health score will help you monitor your health progress. So, find a quiet corner, and let’s get started.

Pike planks are a powerful indicator of shoulder strength, as they engage the deltoids and stabilize the shoulder joint while challenging core stability and control.

Shoulder external rotation

6. Select the position you can perform without changing your pain or discomfort. Complete the test on both sides. Record your lowest score of the two.(Required)

How to test?
